Technical Case Studies

Paint Correction, Decontamination, and Interior Recovery Logs

Technical documentation of recent jobs processed at the Enikepadu facility. These logs detail the specific chemical and mechanical processes used to address common vehicle degradation in the Vijayawada region.

Front-End Clear Coat Leveling and Scratch Removal

Observation: Deep scuffs on the front bumper and hood caused by physical abrasion. Paint depth gauge readings confirmed 115 microns on the hood, allowing for mechanical correction without penetrating the base coat.

Process: We utilized a dual-action polisher with a medium-cut compound to level the clear coat defects. Heat management is critical when working on flexible plastic bumper covers; polisher speeds are kept below 4000 RPM to prevent surface melting. A fine finishing polish was applied to remove compound haze.

Technical Note: Many modern bumpers use flexible plastics. Aggressive compounding can melt the surface if the pad speed is too high or if the panel is not allowed to cool between passes.

Metallic Paint Decontamination and Oxidation Removal

Observation: A blue Toyota Innova Hycross presenting with severe surface oxidation and embedded industrial fallout. Standard washing fails to remove bonded contaminants that cause dullness in dark metallic finishes under high UV exposure.

Process: Applied an iron fallout remover to dissolve metallic particles, followed by synthetic clay bar treatment to extract bonded debris from the clear coat pores. A two-stage machine polish restored the metallic flake depth. Sealed with a silica-based (SiO2) coating, as organic carnauba waxes degrade rapidly in local ambient temperatures exceeding 35°C.

Technical Note: Hard water in this region leaves heavy calcium deposits. If left on dark paint, it etches into the clear coat. Vehicles must be dried immediately after washing or rinsed with filtered water to prevent water spot etching.

Interior Moisture Extraction and Enzyme Odor Treatment

Observation: Persistent damp odor following heavy rainfall, indicating moisture trapped beneath the carpet padding where surface vacuums cannot reach.

Process: Front seating was removed to access the carpet perimeter. A heated carpet extractor was used to pull trapped water from the underlying acoustic padding. Fabrics were treated with an enzyme-based cleaner to break down organic bacteria causing the odor, rather than masking it with aerosol fragrances.

Technical Note: Modern floorboards house extensive wiring harnesses and control modules. Prolonged moisture exposure risks corrosion on ECU connectors, leading to intermittent electrical faults. Thorough structural drying is critical for long-term reliability.

Workshop Operating Notes

Vehicles requiring severe paint correction or interior moisture extraction typically remain in the bay for 48 to 72 hours to allow for proper chemical curing and structural drying. Rushing these processes compromises the longevity of the repair. We limit corrective intake to maintain strict quality control and ensure adequate curing times for ceramic and silica sealants.
