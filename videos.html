Hyundai Verna: Full Dismantle & Repaint

This Verna was fully dismantled down to the skeleton and every part was repainted for a complete, factory-fresh finish. We also replaced the front bumper, headlights, and fog lamps for a "new car" look, and finished the grille in premium glossy black.

We also applied 3-layer interior damping on the iron sheet, the middle layer, and the door pads, plus damping foam at the clips for a heavier, more solid door-close feel and better cabin comfort. See our full car painting services.

MG Hector: Sunroof Rust Restoration

This MG Hector had severe rust around the sunroof, bad enough that rainwater was leaking into the cabin. We cut out the rusted section, fabricated a new panel, and installed it using MIG welding, then primed, puttied, and refinished it with in-house mixed 2K paint before reassembling the vehicle. Learn about our 2K and Glasurit paint systems.
